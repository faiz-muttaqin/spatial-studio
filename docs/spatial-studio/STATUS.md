### 2026-11-25: free-space carving outline (R2 of ALGORITHM_STUDY.md)

### 2026-11-25: R4 review fixes (same branch and PR)

- Cache and state: Rebuild drops the cache entry (forced run, no delay). A result is stored with its key and drawn only for a matching scan, layer and transform, so a stale map never shows while the next loads; a cache hit skips the delay and progress.
