### 2026-11-25: free-space carving outline (R2 of ALGORITHM_STUDY.md)

- Branch `fix/cool-booth-bbb6ba` from `3b2f11b0`. Commit 1 unprojects depth with integer pixel indices (no +0.5), the ARCore raw depth sample convention; commit 2 pins it in CAPTURE_CONTRACT.md "Frames and clocks".
- New `packages/core/src/capture/free-space-carving.ts` (pure): Plan3D's carving method (`extract.ts` at commit `8d0692f4a496`) adapted to 160x90 raw depth with confidence. Floor and ceiling by height percentiles cross-checked with the given plane and the depth histogram peak; wall band from 1.5 m above the floor to 0.15 m under the ceiling; 5 cm grid, occupied at 2 or more hits, free at 3 or more rays that stop at occupied cells; closing (without bridging walls), hole fill, opening, doorway neck cut keeping the part with most camera positions, trace, simplify, and a two-pass Manhattan snap (length-weighted 4θ vote, stub and jog removal, sides over 12 degrees off the axes keep their angle, no snap without a clear vote). Bounded: at most 500 000 rays (pixel step adapts), at most 120 frames, grid at most 500 cells a side.
- Glass and honesty: free space is kept only within 1.5 m of the convex hull of the walked positions and within 0.75 m of floor depth saw (patches under 0.6 m² excepted), applied after hole fill. This limits a glass wall's reach but does not stop it: floor seen through glass can still add up to 1.5 m past the hull, and the edge there is flagged. Each edge reports wall support, free space past it, and the gap to floor planes; free space 0.5 to 3 m past an edge, or a floor plane stopping 1 m or more short, sets `likelyGlass` and low confidence. No carved edge is above medium; the proposal says it is not measured.
- `room-proposal.ts`: carving is the third source (edge basis `carved`, method `carved`, `depth.carving` summary). Rule: carving never used when it crosses itself, leaves walked-path points outside (beyond positions the neck cut dropped on purpose) or is mostly glass-flagged; used when planes and lines give no outline or leave assumed, floor-boundary or low edges; otherwise the line outline is kept and the overlap reported. Unknowns and `suggestedScanTransform` behaviour unchanged.
- Existing glass and sparse-coverage tests were adjusted: the proposal may now carry `carved` edges where it had `assumed` or `floor-boundary` ones; the far boundary must still stay inside the glass.
- Test helper moved to `synthetic-depth.test-helpers.ts` (excluded from the build) so both depth suites share it. New tests: rectangle, L-shape, rotated, doorway into a second room, glass with far points, sparse coverage, bounds, and the selection rule.
- Review round (PR #13): with realistic noise (range error 2 to 6% of distance plus 1 cm, 20% dropout, 40% low-confidence pixels, hand wander) the first version shrank outlines inwards while rating edges medium. Fixed: occupancy and edge fits use only strong wall-band hits (confidence 100 or more, range 4.5 m or less), rays use confidence 32 or more up to 6 m, every strong pixel is kept and only the rays are subsampled; frames are chosen by pose coverage (0.5 m by 30 degrees cells) and frames over 30 m from the median pose are dropped; each edge is refitted to the median of strong hits within 0.3 m (three rounds) and support is measured against that line; the free-space reach is the walked hull grown 1.5 m; the doorway cut is not applied when it drops over 15% of positions; closing radius 3, stub and jog 0.45 m, snap tolerance 15 degrees, a second simplify after the snap. An edge is rated low when under 0.5 m, not refitted, glass-flagged, or when the carved area is under 80% of the walked hull's area. Selection vetoes a carved outline with over 12 corners and under half its edges medium, and, against a line outline at least half backed, one that agrees under 70% or has under 80% of its area. The path-outside excuse for dropped positions is capped at 10%.
- Synthetic noisy results (5 x 4 m, 20 m²; L-shape 18 m²): rectangle 20.0 m² at 2% and 4%, L-shape 18.1 and 17.1 m²; at 6% the rectangle collapses to a 4 m² outline whose edges are all rated low, and the selection rule would not use it.

Both now keep the line outline (4 corners, low and assumed edges, as before): scan 1 because the carved outline crosses itself, scan 2 because it has 14 corners with 5 of 14 edges medium. Carving is therefore not the default on either real scan. About 0.35 to 0.5 s a proposal.
- Limits: no ground truth for the real scans; neck cut can drop a legitimate narrow arm (reported in the notes); carved glass detection depends on floor depth and floor planes; two-room or multi-level walks are not modelled.

### 2026-11-25: room proposal from raw depth (no vertical planes)

- Branch `fix/sharp-sanderson-ae2f84` from `b5dc5431`. `packages/core/src/capture/depth-walls.ts` unprojects raw depth through each depth keyframe's intrinsics and its rgb keyframe's pose (OpenGL axes, z-depth), keeps points between floor and ceiling, flattens them to the floor plane, fits lines with a seeded RANSAC (non-orthogonal allowed) and adds the floor planes' outer boundary as weaker lines. Candidates whose line the walked path crosses are rejected; lines hidden behind a nearer boundary are set aside as seen-through; walls are cut where they run on behind a likely-glass boundary. Floor and ceiling levels come from the depth height histogram when there is no plane set (reported as such). Work is bounded (at most 120 frames, pixel step 2, 5 cm grid).
- `propose_room_from_scan` now reads depth and the walked path. Planes are used alone when they close an outline; otherwise depth and floor-boundary lines are added. Edge bases: `plane-estimate`, `depth-fit`, `floor-boundary`, `assumed`, `manual-measurement` (`plane-estimate` kept, not renamed, to keep existing consumers working). Depth-fit edges are never rated high; edges carry `evidenceIds`, `likelyGlass` and `seenBeyond`. Assumed edges that leave the path outside are pushed outward and reported. Two parallel walls give a quad with assumed ends.
- Orientation check: on both local scans the OpenGL convention matched the ARCore point cloud best (76% and 81% of depth points within 15 cm of a cloud point, against at most 68% and 71% for flipped, transposed or ray-length variants) and gave sharp floor levels. A left-right mirror differs by about 14 and 24 points, so the evidence is real but not overwhelming.
- Real-scan validation (private, not committed): neither local scan has a plane set, so floor and ceiling came from the depth histogram. Scan 1: a four-edge outline enclosing the walked path, three depth-fit edges and one assumed edge flagged likely glass with depth running about 5 m past it. Scan 2: two long parallel wall groups with both ends assumed; 14 of 1632 path points fall outside, which the proposal reports. Neither is verified against the real room.
- Floor choice without wall planes: the largest upward plane is the floor (no longer the lowest); a second large upward plane (at least 40% of its area) at another level is reported as a possible step. A given floor is cross-checked against the depth height histogram and a mismatch over 0.25 m is reported. Tested with synthetic plane sets shaped like the owner's live scan.
- `suggestedScanTransform` on the proposal: scan-node position moved in y so the detected floor sits at level y 0 (x, z, rotation, scale unchanged, so the polygon stays valid). A suggestion only; north alignment is not included.
- Review fixes (same PR): with no floor planes, the floor depth saw (away from wall feet) stands in as the floor boundary, so glass with no floor beyond it is flagged, and every depth-fit edge far from the walked path (over 4 m) or without floor seen up to it is rated low; the proposal says glass cannot be ruled out. A floor plane that ends over 1 m short of a measured wall, with floor seen between, is reported as ambiguous (wall used, both positions in `unknowns`), not as glass. Merged lines keep only real plane ids in `evidencePlaneIds` and count as plane-based only when planes cover 60% of them. `suggestedScanTransform` carries `confidence` and `floorBasis` and is low when the floor is estimated from depth or depth disagrees. The depth-histogram floor is the strongest peak; a distinct lower peak is reported as a possible step (not covered by a test). A doorway the path walks through splits the wall instead of rejecting it. Depth frames whose keyframe was not tracked are skipped, and the proposal loads the trajectory without the point cloud. `dropCompetingLines` already treated plane-only lines as before (rank only orders mixed lines), so it was not gated.
- Limits: the depth-fit outline is a hypothesis; a floor plane that stops more than 1 m short of a solid wall is read as glass (heuristic); a path that legitimately crosses a wall line (a doorway) rejects that line; depth timestamps are not matched to pose beyond the rgb keyframe link; tall furniture along a wall can pass as wall.
